Welcome to
The Postcard Diaries

Hi, I'm Bianca, and this blog follows my partner John and me as we travel around Japan! Just like the postcards we collect from every place we visit, it's our way of holding on to the little moments from our trips and sharing them with you. Our trips have taken us from quiet temples and matcha in Uji to shopping in bustling Tokyo, and from the bright blue ponds to snowy Mt Asahidake in Hokkaido. Here we share the experiences we loved, the food we tasted and our honest tips for planning your own trip.
